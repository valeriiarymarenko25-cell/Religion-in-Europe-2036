                                                      **Religion in Europe, 2036**  
                               **What will religion in Europe look like in 2036 – who will believe, who will belong, and who will practise?**  
       In my opinion, there is likely to be fewer people who believe in God, identify with a particular religion, or attend church regularly in Ukraine by 2036\. Based on my observations and experience, the majority of my close people, as well as people on the internet, have become atheists or started attending church less frequently, even those who used to do it often just a few years ago. In addition, I’ve noticed that some people have a skeptical attitude to religious organisations.  
       However, I believe that there might be huge differences between younger and older generations, since older people already have their religious beliefs and traditions established. In contrast, younger people, based on my observations, are becoming less interested in religion or faith.  
       Moreover, the existence of wars, hunger and other forms of human suffering can make people either doubt the existence of God, or make them believe even more. Some people may question the existence of God and lose their faith watching horrible situations happen, while others may turn to religion for hope. As a result, some people can become religious, and others move further away from it.   
       Nevertheless, I reckon the overall level of religiosity in Ukraine will decline by 2036\.
